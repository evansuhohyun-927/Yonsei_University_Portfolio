**6.2.1** (a)⇒(b): T(x)·T(y) = ¼(‖T(x) + T(y)‖² − ‖T(x) − T(y)‖²) = ¼(‖T(x + y)‖² − ‖T(x − y)‖²) = ¼(‖x + y‖² − ‖x − y‖²) = x·y. (b)⇒(a): ‖T(x)‖ = √(T(x)·T(x)) = √(x·x) = ‖x‖. <sub>〔Ch6 p.25〕</sub>

**6.2.3** (a) Aᵀ(Aᵀ)ᵀ = AᵀA = I이므로 (Aᵀ)⁻¹ = (Aᵀ)ᵀ. (b) (A⁻¹)ᵀ = (Aᵀ)ᵀ = A = (A⁻¹)⁻¹. (c) (AB)⁻¹ = B⁻¹A⁻¹ = BᵀAᵀ = (AB)ᵀ. (d) det(Aᵀ)det(A) = (det A)² = det(I) = 1. <sub>〔Ch6 p.28〕</sub>

**Ex 4** (a) det = ½ + ½ = 1 → R<sub>θ</sub>. cos θ = sin θ = 1/√2 → θ = 45°. (b) det = −½ − ½ = −1 → H<sub>θ/2</sub>. cos θ = sin θ = 1/√2 → θ = 45°, 반사선은 22.5°. `보완`
